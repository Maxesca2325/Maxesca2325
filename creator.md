## Channel (since 2026-11-15)
- Niche: **French interactive horror "protocole" stories**, fiction, told in the 2nd person, multi-part series where the comments choose what happens next. Reference account (format only): TikTok @suisleprotocole. Style numbers: `styles/protocole.md`.
- Audience: French-speaking teens and young adults, mainly France, who like creepypasta, rules/"protocol" horror and choosing the hero's fate.
- Core promise: "I'm the one inside the story, and I decide if I survive."
- Engine: HyperFrames (`hf/`), animated cartoon look, sharper and smoother than the reference (see the "Ours" column in the style file).
- Previous niche (true history, `episodes/ep2` Saint-Pierre) is shelved, not deleted.

## Script rules
- French, 2nd person "tu", **present tense**. Short sentences. Simple words.
- First line, spoken at 0.0 s: an ordinary night situation + one wrong detail, often with a precise odd time ("2h41."). No intro.
- One new wrong detail per line. At least one written rule found in the world ("Règle numéro 1 : …").
- Pace ~2.8–3.0 words/s (calm, whispery). 90–130 s per part (≈ 260–380 words).
- End of part 1/2: "Tu dois choisir." + two clear options + "Vote en commentaire. La réponse la plus votée décide de la suite. Épisode 2 demain. Abonne-toi pour ne pas le rater." (vary the wording a little).
- Each later part opens with the voted choice ("Vous avez choisi : …") and ends on a new choice or a twist ending with a "Règle" the viewer learns.
- **Original stories only.** Never reuse the reference account's stories, lines, characters or settings combo (cursed plane, neighbour waving, girlfriend selfie, etc.).
- No fact-check table needed (fiction), but anything presented as real (places, laws) must be true or clearly invented.
- Keep `episodes/<serie>/choices.md`: each part, the two options, the winning vote, what it leads to.
